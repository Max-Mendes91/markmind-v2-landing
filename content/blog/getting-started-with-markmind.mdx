# Getting Started with MarkMind

MarkMind is an AI-powered Chrome extension that organizes your bookmarks into logical folders. Here is how to get started in under five minutes.

---

## Step 1: Install the Extension

Head to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/markmind/bdobgdkpeffdbonfpokgkbncgnbnjnoo) and click **Add to Chrome**. The extension is free and open source.

## Step 2: Add Your API Key

MarkMind uses your own OpenAI API key to power the AI categorization. Open the extension popup, navigate to **Settings**, and paste your key. Your key never leaves your browser — everything runs locally.

## Step 3: Select Bookmarks to Organize

Click on any bookmark folder, select the bookmarks you want to sort, and hit **Organize**. MarkMind will analyze each bookmark and suggest the best folder for it.

---

## Tips for Best Results

- **Start with a single folder** to see how the AI categorizes your bookmarks
- **Review the suggestions** before confirming — you are always in control
- **Use bulk mode** for hundreds of bookmarks at once
- **Try different folders** — the AI adapts its suggestions based on your existing folder structure

> MarkMind respects your existing folder structure. It suggests — it never takes control away from you.

## What Makes MarkMind Different

Unlike other bookmark managers, MarkMind does not require an account or cloud sync. Your data stays in your browser. The AI runs on-demand with your own API key, which means:

- **No subscription fees** — pay only for the AI calls you make
- **Complete privacy** — bookmarks never leave your device
- **Open source** — inspect the code yourself on [GitHub](https://github.com/migsilva89/MarkMind)

That is it. Your bookmarks are now organized.
